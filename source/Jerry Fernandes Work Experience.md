## Jerry Fernandes MA thesis

Title: A study on meaning in life and coping with the fear of death.

Abstract:  
Multiple theories concerning the meaning of life have surfaced within philosophy over  
centuries as well as in the last few decades, with the discussion around the term steadily  
growing. Within this project I wish to provide a fresh perspective towards the meaning of life  
by trying to explore the connection between meaningful experiences and managing death  
anxiety. I have considered a very intuitive approach towards both: understanding what counts  
as meaningful or not, as well as towards understanding what death anxiety is and why and  
how it must be dealt with. Drawing from Terror Management Theory (TMT) I wish to  
demonstrate that developing a sense of self transcendence, or in other words, developing a  
sense of having evaded one’s death can be a meaningful experience. I do so by discussing  
four primary ways of discovering meaning in life namely Spirituality (mainly consisting of,  
but not limited to religious experience), Love (for other beings), Legacy (the tendency to  
contribute in a significant fashion through one’s work) and Cultural affiliation. I then discuss  
how these ways of discovering meaning invoke a sense of transcendence within the  
individual and finally conclude with establishing a link between transcendence and meaning.  
This project serves as an introduction to the idea that managing our death anxiety can provide  
us with meaning in life.
