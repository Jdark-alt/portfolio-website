# Headline and standfirst options

All of these are live on the preview: open **Preview controls** (bottom right) and pick a headline and standfirst, or add `?h=h3&s=s2` to the link to send someone a specific version. The text lives in `src/content/home.yaml`, so editing a line there changes it on the site.

## Headlines

The first part (lead) is set quieter; the second part (main) is the statement.

| # | Lead | Main | Why it might work | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| h1 | Six years explaining B2B software. | Now I build it. | Clear career turn in two beats. | You find it cheesy; "build" could suggest you write code. |
| h2 | I used to explain products. | Now I decide what goes into them. | Names what a PM actually does (deciding). | Longer; less punchy. |
| h3 | I don't write the code. | I own everything else. | Upfront about AI-built work; confident. | Opens on a negative; some readers may stop at line one. |
| h4 | | I frame the problem, make the call, write the spec and ship it. | Process first; matches How I work below it. | Reads more like a summary than a headline. |
| h5 | Product management, | shown with the working. | Promises evidence, which the site delivers. | "Shown with the working" is a British/Indian school idiom; may not land everywhere. |
| h6 | Six years making ERP make sense. | Now I make the product. | Leans on Odoo/ERP; strongest for Cin7-type roles. | Narrower; "make the product" is vague. |
| h7 | Two products. | Every decision on the record. | Short; points at the evidence rather than the career change. | Doesn't say you're a PM. |
| h8 | Product manager. | Two live products, 15+ specs, 25 builds in five weeks. | Scannable; numbers a recruiter remembers. | CV-like; low personality. |

**My pick:** h2 for a general PM audience, h6 for ERP and inventory companies. h3 is the most memorable but also the most divisive, so it's the one worth testing with people.

**How to collect opinions:** send two or three people the same link with different `?h=` values and ask one question: "After reading only the headline, what do you think this person does?" The headline whose answer comes closest to "product manager" wins.

## Standfirsts (the paragraph under the headline)

- **s1 (default):** what the two products are, then "I frame the problem, make the calls and write the specs. AI coding agents write the code."
- **s2:** starts from the content career ("For six years I explained B2B and ERP software…") and ends with "including what broke".
- **s3:** the shortest; ownership in one sentence, then "Everything below links to the evidence."

s1 pairs with any headline. s2 works best with h3, h7 or h8, which don't mention the career change themselves. s3 works best with h1 or h6, which already do.
